Bio

Desmond Zhao is a business development and partnerships leader based in Shanghai, with 15+ years in enterprise software, cloud and developer tools. He started his career in 2011 and worked at Dell, GE, AWS and VMware before JetBrains, where he built the China business for a global developer-tools company from zero and connected it with China's leading tech companies and AI model vendors. He is now exploring new work at the intersection of data and frontier AI labs. Outside work he cycles, hunts for good food, and brings friends and family together.
